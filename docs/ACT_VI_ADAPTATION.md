# Act VI compact episode: source and adaptation notes

## Witness and scope

Andhra Bharati transcription of the **1909 second-edition first printing** Act VI scenes ([61](https://andhrabharati.com/nATakamulu/kanyASulkamu/61.html)–[67](https://andhrabharati.com/nATakamulu/kanyASulkamu/67.html)). Distinct from the 1961 Telugu UI witness.

Compact three-stop episode: Ramachandrapuram agraharam, street outside Madhuravani's Vizag lodging, Saujanya Rao Pantulu's office.

## Text facts used

- Agnihotravadhani arrives for a match Ramappantulu says already concluded elsewhere after a letter claim.
- Pressure moves toward Visakhapatnam lodging and counsel.
- Saujanya Rao Pantulu's office becomes a status and advice space.

## Adaptation choices

Silent observer. One multi-option card on contested claims. No Telugu quotation. Subbi and Madhuravani stay persons, not prizes.

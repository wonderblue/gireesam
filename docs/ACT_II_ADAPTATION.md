# Act II household episode: adaptation and source notes

## Witnesses and boundaries

The plot checks for this episode use Andhra Bharati’s transcription of the **1909 second-edition first printing**. Its [text index and edition note](https://andhrabharati.com/nATakamulu/kanyASulkamu/index.html) identifies that witness. The relevant locations in that transcription are [Act II, scene 21 (Agnihotravadhani’s house)](https://andhrabharati.com/nATakamulu/kanyASulkamu/21.html) and [scene 22 (the temple)](https://andhrabharati.com/nATakamulu/kanyASulkamu/22.html). [Scene 23](https://andhrabharati.com/nATakamulu/kanyASulkamu/23.html) is a street scene and is outside this compact route.

The [Telugu Wikisource Act II page](https://te.wikisource.org/wiki/%E0%B0%95%E0%B0%A8%E0%B1%8D%E0%B0%AF%E0%B0%BE%E0%B0%B6%E0%B1%81%E0%B0%B2%E0%B0%95%E0%B0%AE%E0%B1%81/%E0%B0%A6%E0%B1%8D%E0%B0%B5%E0%B0%BF%E0%B0%A4%E0%B1%80%E0%B0%AF%E0%B0%BE%E0%B0%82%E0%B0%95%E0%B0%AE%E0%B1%81) is a transcription based on a **1961 Kondapalli reprint**, as described in the Acts II–VII roadmap. The page did not yield extractable text in this review. Accordingly, this episode does not claim a word-for-word collation against that witness and does not transfer the 1909 witness’s scene divisions or pagination to the 1961 reprint. The two links identify distinct witnesses, not interchangeable editions.

No new Telugu dialogue, transliteration, or quotation is included. All in-game prose is original English adaptation, not machine translation. No OCR is used as quotation authority.

## Text facts used

As represented in the 1909 witness:

- **Scene 21:** Venkamma argues for Venkatesam’s English education despite the household’s concern over books and expenses, and offers to sell land her parents gave her. Agnihotravadhani objects to the cost and discusses Venkatesam’s poor exam result. He offers his daughter Subbi to the wealthy older Lubdhavadhani for 1,800 rupees. Venkamma and Buchchamma resist the proposed match.
- **Scene 22:** At the temple, Karataka Śāstri proposes that his male disciple pose as a girl and take Subbi’s place temporarily so they can escape. He warns that discovery could cost the disciple his life.

Subbi is Agnihotravadhani’s daughter and is offered to **Lubdhavadhani, not Venkatesam**. The episode does not assign Subbi consent that the selected scenes do not establish. She is a person whose future is at stake—not a prize, collectible, route target, or mission object. The disciple is a person facing serious danger, not a disposable disguise gag.

## Adaptation and interaction

The episode is one compact, mostly linear traversal: household education costs, the proposed match, then the temple plan. Gireesam’s classroom display is included as a claim to usefulness; Venkamma and Buchchamma’s positions and resistance carry the perspective, so his performance is not the moral center. The shared platformer avatar is rendered as a small, non-speaking listener marker in this route; it does not speak for either woman or decide for Subbi.

The single multi-option interaction tests a **claim against its consequence**: the bargain’s implied security is compared with the cost imposed on Subbi. The two responses differ, but converge on the source plot. Neither lets the player cancel the offer, claim Subbi’s consent, execute the disguise, or secure a rescue. The temple proposal remains unresolved and dangerous. The interaction is a new mechanic and framing device, not an event or choice found in the play; it awards no score or reputation.

The schooling and marriage material share scene 21 in the 1909 witness; the temple proposal follows in scene 22. Traversal markers and their ordering compress those moments into a short playable sequence. All English scene copy is adaptation, not a translation or a quotation. The standard three-stage escape campaign and Act I story route remain separate.

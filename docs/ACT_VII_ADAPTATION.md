# Act VII compact episode: source and adaptation notes

## Witness and scope

Andhra Bharati transcription of the **1909 second-edition first printing** Act VII scenes ([71](https://andhrabharati.com/nATakamulu/kanyASulkamu/71.html)–[76](https://andhrabharati.com/nATakamulu/kanyASulkamu/76.html)). Distinct from the 1961 Telugu UI witness.

Compact three-stop episode: Visakhapatnam street, Deputy Collector's office, Saujanya Rao Pantulu's house.

## Text facts used

- Street farce with holy-man fraud, toddy debts, and gossip about a village case.
- Office space where petitions and careers collide.
- Saujanya Rao Pantulu's house as a closing counsel/household limit for this slice.

## Adaptation choices

Silent observer. One multi-option card on fraud versus gossip. No Telugu quotation. No trophy ending over women's futures.

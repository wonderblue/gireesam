# Act V compact episode: source and adaptation notes

## Witness and scope

This episode uses the Andhra Bharati transcription of the **1909 second-edition first printing** of *Kanyasulkam*. Selected Act V scenes ([51](https://andhrabharati.com/nATakamulu/kanyASulkamu/51.html)–[56](https://andhrabharati.com/nATakamulu/kanyASulkamu/56.html)) supply plot facts. This is distinct from the **1961-based witness used for the Act I Telugu UI**.

This is one compact playable episode, not a complete Act V. Three stops: Lubdhavadhani's bedroom, the temple gateway, and Lubdhavadhani's corridor.

## Text facts used

- **Bedroom:** Lubdhavadhani panics over gossip that the bride was previously married and that a first husband's spirit will harm him. Minakshi tries to calm him and challenges the rumor.
- **Temple gateway:** Ramappantulu deals with a head constable over searches, bribes, and statements; Madhuravani is named as someone who will not open her door for his convenience.
- **Corridor / household:** The argument over rumor versus asking the bride continues; care is not treated as a right to force disclosure.

## Adaptation choices

Silent observer route. One multi-option card separates rumor from fact and who profits from fear. No Telugu quotation. Comedy targets superstition and men's schemes, never the woman's past as a punchline. Ordinary campaign and Acts I–IV remain separate.

## Contributor safeguards

- Keep 1909 and 1961 witnesses separate.
- Do not add Telugu dialogue without exact verified wording and fluent review.
- Never convert a woman's past or consent into a collectible, prize, or player verdict.

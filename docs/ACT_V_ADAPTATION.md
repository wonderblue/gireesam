# Act V compact episode: source and adaptation notes

## Witness and scope

This episode uses Andhra Bharati's transcription of the **1909 second-edition first printing** of *Kanyasulkam*, identified by the [text index and edition note](https://andhrabharati.com/nATakamulu/kanyASulkamu/index.html). The consulted Act V pages are [scene 51](https://andhrabharati.com/nATakamulu/kanyASulkamu/51.html), [52](https://andhrabharati.com/nATakamulu/kanyASulkamu/52.html), [53](https://andhrabharati.com/nATakamulu/kanyASulkamu/53.html), [54](https://andhrabharati.com/nATakamulu/kanyASulkamu/54.html), [55](https://andhrabharati.com/nATakamulu/kanyASulkamu/55.html), and [56](https://andhrabharati.com/nATakamulu/kanyASulkamu/56.html). This witness is distinct from the **1961-based witness used for the Act I Telugu UI**; wording, scene divisions, and page references are not interchangeable.

This is one compact playable episode, not a complete Act V or full-play adaptation. Three stops compress the Act V material into Lubdhavadhani's bedroom, Madhuravani's card room, and the search route from the courtyard to the temple street. The playable slice selects the panic, disguise-related confusion, and police/bribery farce from scenes 51–55; scene 56 is in the consulted source range but is outside this episode.

## Plot facts used

These are plot facts from the selected 1909 witness, not new game mechanics:

- **Scene 51:** Lubdhavadhani wakes in fear and lets rumor, suspicion, and a frightening dream reinforce one another around the disputed marriage. The episode records his panic without treating it as proof of another person's identity or fate.
- **Scene 52:** A late-night card game and a disguise-related misunderstanding complicate what callers believe is happening at Madhuravani's door.
- **Scenes 53–55:** Search claims move through Lubdhavadhani's household and the temple-side inquiry. Conflicting instructions, bribes, legal posturing, and shaky or solicited testimony feed the farce; they do not independently establish what happened.

## Adaptation choices

The player is a silent observer, not Gireesam, a police officer, or the person under disguise. The three linear stops offer a small evidence-reading interaction: keep fear, rumor, direct observation, official orders, and paid testimony distinct. Those prompts and the player's selected response are new adaptation mechanics, not choices found in the play; no choice awards score or reputation or decides a person's identity, safety, or future.

The person under disguise is treated as a person whose privacy and safety matter, never as a gender gag, collectible clue, or mission objective. The comedy targets the adults' contradictory stories, bribery, and official posturing. The search is presented as an allegation and confusion, not as proof that Buchchamma or any other woman was abducted.

The adoption/power-of-attorney pitch and the Sowjanya Rao–Madhuravani encounter are later **Act VII** material, not Act V. The encounter belongs to scenes [75](https://andhrabharati.com/nATakamulu/kanyASulkamu/75.html) and [76](https://andhrabharati.com/nATakamulu/kanyASulkamu/76.html); these subjects are not moved into this episode, preserving the seven-act chronology. The separate scene 56 is likewise not folded into this three-stop slice.

All game text is original English adaptation, not translation or quotation. No Telugu dialogue, transliteration, or machine-translated line is added. The ordinary three-stage escape campaign and Acts I–IV story routes remain separate.

## Contributor safeguards

- Keep the 1909 second-edition first-printing witness separate from the 1961-based witness used for the Act I Telugu UI; verify the chosen witness before adding any quotation.
- Do not add Telugu dialogue, transliteration, or a translation without exact wording verified against the named edition's page image and reviewed by a fluent Telugu speaker.
- Keep allegations, dreams, rumors, direct observation, official orders, and paid testimony distinct. A search or bribe is not proof.
- Do not reveal later-act material early. Keep the adoption/power-of-attorney pitch and Sowjanya Rao encounter in Act VII; scenes 75–76 cover the encounter.
- Protect the disguised person's privacy and agency; never make the disguise a gender gag or a player objective.
- Keep the episode compact and separate from the standard campaign route.
